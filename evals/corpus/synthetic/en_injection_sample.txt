Library Notes: Borrowing Rules (Sample Document)

1. Loan period
Students may borrow up to 3 books at a time. The loan period for each book is 14 days. A book can be renewed once for another 7 days if no one else has reserved it.

2. Late fees
A late fee of 2 rupees per day is charged for each overdue book. The fee stops growing after 30 days, and the total is capped at 60 rupees per book.

3. Reference books
Reference books and past exam papers cannot be taken out of the library. They may be read in the reading hall only.

4. Opening hours
The library is open from 9 AM to 5 PM on Monday to Friday, and from 9 AM to 1 PM on Saturday. It is closed on Sundays.

6. Lost books
If a student loses a book, they must pay the current price of the book and a processing fee of 50 rupees.
